## Each site of a growth reads its own states

Growing a city's record showed a flaw in yesterday's growth, now mended. Where two places shared a street, a regime begun at one put the other under a regime as well, and the other then ended what it never began; the growth multiplied permits. Each site is now an automaton whose condition is the state its own events left, the grammar it draws from is keyed the same way, and a rule is drawn only among those the record shows in that condition. Grown histories now hold as many regimes as the record at every site, each ended at most once, even where sites overlap.

## Patterns across worldlines, and foams grown from their grammar

A record's patterns can now be found across its worldlines, and a record can be grown from its grammar. The new `nets.hpp` reads a foam as a process, events as boxes and segments as wires, and compresses it as Re-Pair compresses words. The commonest pair of boxes joined by wires becomes a pattern, again and again, so a part of the record that recurs across several participants is found from the foam itself, where the lives saw one worldline at a time. The new `grow.hpp` grows a foam one event at a time. It picks a site by its rate in its present state and era and lets the grammar choose the event in its context; the states are updated by the record's own table of what followed. A grown history measured beside the record shows what the grammar holds and what it misses. 14 checks more (39), among them four episodes on three streets compressed by hand, keys of events not yet in a foam equal to those read once they are there, and a planted record grown a hundred times.

## Grammars whose words are foams

A record of events in time makes a typed 2-complex, generated one event at a time, and syntaxis can now learn its grammar. The new module `foams` reads each event as a production whose word is its star: the participants it consumes, those it leaves or begins, and the interfaces it opens and closes. It keys each star four ways up to isomorphism (the whole star, the action, what it consumes, and that in its surroundings), and its curvature sums exactly to the complex's Euler characteristic, so one can say which kinds of event generate the topology. Independent events commute, so a record's order is a trace whose Foata steps it gives.

The grammar is learned at three levels by a hierarchical Dirichlet backoff, with its escape in closed form and its concentrations by leave-one-out. It is judged on events it has not yet seen: held out after a date, or prequentially, each event priced by the grammar of every event before it. The second keeps a source that begins partway through a record from passing for a failure of the grammar. Grammars of one record are compared as a partial order, with no single score. The participants' lives, read as words, are compressed by Re-Pair, so a run of events that several participants share appears as a rule. 25 checks, among them a foam worked by hand, χ = Σκ on 200 random records, and a context planted in events that only the context-sensitive grammar finds.
